# My-Favorite-Songs Q1 Seatwork 1

My Top 3 Spotify Songs

1. Not Another Song About Love

"Not Another Song About Love" by Hollywood Ending means exploring a messy, painful, and toxic relationship where love and hate exist at the same time.

2. Paparazzi

"Paparazzi" is about the intense struggle to balance personal love with the consuming desire for fame.

3. Too Little, Too Late

"Too Little, Too Late" by Laufey explores the deep pain of unrequited love, missed timing, and the bitter regret of realizing a chance at romance has gone forever.
